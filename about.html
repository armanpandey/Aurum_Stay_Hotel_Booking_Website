About Aurum Stay

A story of warmth, hospitality, and a passion for making every guest feel at home.

Our Story

Built on a Simple Belief

AurumStay was founded in 2010 with one simple idea: every traveller deserves to feel genuinely welcome. Not just checked in, but truly cared for.

What started as a small boutique property has grown into a beloved 60-room hotel, known for personal service, thoughtful design, and an atmosphere that feels more like a home than a hotel.

We believe luxury doesn't have to mean cold and formal. It can be warm, personal, and real.
